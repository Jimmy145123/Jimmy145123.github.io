<div align="justify">
I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Yonsei University and an undergraduate researcher at the ML3 Lab, advised by Prof. Jaehyung Kim.
<br/><br/>
My research focuses on understanding and exploiting the internal representations of AI systems so that they can operate efficiently, adaptively, and reliably under real-world constraints. I have worked on training-free structured pruning of large language models, adaptive model routing for LLM serving, and cross-model KV-cache transfer. I am also extending these questions to machine learning for biomedical data through a collaboration on wearable cardiac ultrasound.

Research
======
**Training-free, task-aware structured pruning (DIET).** A pruning method in which each task flags the hidden dimensions it barely uses from a few examples, without training, and the votes are merged into a single global mask for a large language model.

**Adaptive routing and cross-model KV-cache transfer.** With Prof. Xue (Steve) Liu's group at McGill, I work on how LLM serving systems can route requests using cheap, objective-dependent signals, and whether key/value representations from one model can be reused by another to avoid redundant computation.

**Machine learning for wearable cardiac ultrasound.** With DGIST and USC, I study how to formulate patch-placement guidance as a learning problem under noisy signals, patient and device variability, and sparse labels.
